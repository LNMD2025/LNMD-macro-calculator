# TrendSpotter - Hospitality Content Tracker

Track trending reels, posts, and TikToks in the hospitality industry to stay ahead with content creation.

## Features

### Content Discovery
- **Multi-Platform Support**: Track content from Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook, and YouTube Shorts
- **Industry Categories**: Filter by Cafe, Pizza, Restaurant, Bar, Bakery, and Food Truck
- **Real-time Statistics**: View engagement rates, views, likes, shares, and growth metrics
- **Trending Hashtags**: See which hashtags are performing best in hospitality

### Analytics & Insights
- **Best Posting Times**: Know when to post for maximum engagement
- **Content Format Performance**: Understand which content types work best
- **Growth Tracking**: See which content is gaining momentum fastest
- **Engagement Rates**: Compare performance across platforms

### Productivity Tools
- **Bookmark Content**: Save inspiring posts for later reference
- **Export Reports**: Download trend data as JSON for your team
- **Content Ideas**: Get AI-suggested content ideas based on current trends
- **Sort & Filter**: Find exactly what you need quickly

## How to Use

1. **Browse Trending Content**: View the latest viral posts in hospitality
2. **Filter by Platform**: Focus on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, or YouTube
3. **Filter by Category**: Narrow down to your specific niche (cafe, pizza, etc.)
4. **Save Inspiration**: Bookmark content that inspires you
5. **Export Data**: Download reports to share with your team
6. **Check Analytics**: Review best posting times and content formats

## Content Categories

- **Cafe/Coffee Shop**: Latte art, specialty drinks, cozy vibes
- **Pizza**: Dough stretching, oven shots, slice reveals
- **Restaurant**: Plating, behind-the-scenes, chef life
- **Bar**: Cocktail making, flair bartending, nightlife
- **Bakery**: Bread baking, cake decorating, pastry making
- **Food Truck**: Day in the life, street food, mobile kitchen

## Trending Content Types

Based on current trends, these content formats perform best:

1. **Short-form video (15-30s)** - 94% engagement
2. **Tutorial/How-to** - 87% engagement
3. **Behind-the-scenes** - 82% engagement
4. **Before/After reveals** - 79% engagement
5. **Carousel posts** - 68% engagement
